Privacy Policy

App: Ghar Kharcha · Last updated: 8 October 2026

Ghar Kharcha helps families track bills, due dates, shared expenses and payments. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it is kept.

1. Information stored on your device

The information you enter, such as your name, family member names, bills, amounts, expenses, payment records, and settings, is stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage. We do not operate servers that receive or keep this information.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, financial account details, or device identifiers. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not create user accounts.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, because we never receive it. If you choose to share a backup file or an Excel export, you are the one sharing it.

4. Notifications

If you turn on bill reminders, the notifications are generated on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app settings or in your phone's notification settings.

5. Backups and exports

Backup files and Excel exports are saved to your device when you download them. Keep them safe, because they contain your household data.

6. Deleting your data

You can erase everything in the app: open Settings, then "Erase all data". Uninstalling the app or clearing the site data in your browser also removes the stored information.

7. Children

Ghar Kharcha is intended for adults who manage household finances. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes how information is handled, for example by adding cloud sync or online payments, we will update this policy and the date above before the change takes effect.

9. Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask questions about your data by contacting us at the address below.
